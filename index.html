What we get called for

Tell us what it's doing

You don't need to know the part name. Describe what happened and we'll tell you on the phone whether it's a repair or a replacement.

Loud bang, door won't lift

Broken spring

The springs carry the weight of the door, not the opener. When one snaps the door gets too heavy to lift and the opener can burn out trying. Don't force it — springs are under real tension.

Remote clicks, nothing moves

Opener issue

Could be the safety sensors knocked out of alignment, a stripped gear, or the logic board. Usually a same-visit fix, and usually cheaper than people expect.

Crooked, scraping, jammed

Door off track

Usually a bent track, a broken roller, or a cable that came off the drum — often after something bumped the door. Leave it where it is and call. Running it again bends more of the track.
