How much does a garage door cost?

Starting prices: $1,450 for a basic 16×7 door, $2,150 mid-range, $2,950 premium insulated, and openers from $570. Final price depends on size, style, insulation and windows. Estimates are free.

Do you offer emergency service?

Yes. Call 602-869-2753. Same-day service is available.

Do you repair broken springs?

Yes. We repair broken springs and also do tune-ups and safety checks.

Do you install garage door openers?

Yes. Opener installs, repairs and reprogramming. Openers start at $570.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Estimates are free.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Work is backed by a 12-month warranty.

What areas do you service?

Phoenix, Mesa, Chandler, Gilbert and Tempe, across the Phoenix metro area.
